# Output Format

## Reframed Challenge

- Outcome:
- Users or stakeholders:
- Constraints:
- Facts and assumptions:

## Selected Methods

For each method, give one sentence explaining why it fits and what distinct role it plays.

## Idea Portfolio

| Idea | Mechanism | Source method | Expected benefit | Main trade-off |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Include a mix of incremental, adjacent, and non-obvious ideas. Merge duplicates before presenting the table.

## Shortlist

Score each dimension from 1 to 5 and explain the evidence briefly. Scores are comparative estimates, not measurements.

| Idea | Impact | Feasibility | Differentiation | Evidence confidence | Time-to-learn | Why it ranks here |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|

Do not use a weighted total unless the user supplies priorities. If priorities are known, state the weights before calculating.

## Recommendation and Experiment

- Recommended concept:
- Why now:
- Critical assumption:
- Smallest reversible experiment:
- Success signal:
- Failure signal:
- Next decision:

## Wild Card

Keep one unusual concept and state the transferable principle it exposes, even when it is not recommended.
